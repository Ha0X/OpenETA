# Support contact contract

These are current scoped tool contracts, not additional robot capabilities. Live schemas and receipts take precedence.

To place an already held object on the table, mark the visible tabletop beside/below it, then use a separate single-arm `move_to` with `support_contact={"mode":"place","surface_mark":"mark_..."}`. Preserve orientation and XY and request a downward target no more than 5 cm away, without waypoints. The Host requires current two-finger attachment evidence and the held object to fit within the authorized radius of the marked table point (20 cm by default). It may shorten the descent to the support surface and stops when contact is measured; inspect `support_contact`, `host_adjustments`, and the images before deciding to open the gripper. `support_contact_reached` is an intentional contact stop, not a claim that the original TCP target or the benchmark task completed. The authorization lasts for this move only, allows at most 1 mm measured support penetration, and does not permit hand/table contact, arbitrary sliding/sweeping, or shared-object manipulation.

When one arm must keep a held object resting on the table while the other arm moves, explicitly set `retain_support=true` inside that arm's `support_contact` placement request. Retention begins only after measured placement contact. Read `active_support_holds` and the authorization changes in `host_adjustments`. The original body/table patch and 1 mm penetration bound remain monitored; the holding wrist must stay within 2 mm and 0.02 rad of its placement pose. Requesting motion of that arm, reauthorizing its grasp contact, or completing gripper release expires the support permission. For lifting away, use the short vertical separating recovery described above.
